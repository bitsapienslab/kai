Parent questions, answered

Is KAI safe for my young person?

Yes. KAI is built with content safety, human review and crisis pathways at every layer, guided by youth development experts.

How is my child's data used and protected?

Data is encrypted, minimally collected, and never sold. Access is strictly controlled and privacy is built in by design.

Can I see everything my child does in KAI?

No — KAI offers supportive oversight, not surveillance. You'll receive meaningful weekly highlights, not private conversations.

What if my child needs extra help?

KAI has clear escalation pathways to trusted adults and professional support whenever it's needed.

How is KAI different from social media?

KAI is designed for growth, not engagement. There's no feed, no likes, and no pressure — just tools that support real development.
