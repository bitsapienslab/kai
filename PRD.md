# PRD — RISE

**Product:** RISE — Youth Development Operating System  
**Version:** V1 controlled pilot  
**Audience:** Young people aged 13–17  
**Format:** Installable mobile PWA  
**Claim:** **Rise above the noise. Build your direction.**

## Product vision

Young people live in a world of increasing options, stimulation and distraction. It is becoming harder to understand what matters and where to invest attention, effort and time. Many also face emotional distress, risky behaviors and dependencies that can compromise identity, autonomy and future development.

RISE helps young people distinguish signal from noise, develop agency, purpose, character skills and stamina, and progressively build the ability to make good choices, act with intention and ask for help.

RISE is not positioned as an AI companion or a wrapper around an LLM. Kai is the conversational entry point. The long-term product value is a structured, longitudinal development system that connects experiences, behavior, evidence, safety and outcomes.

## Core product model

RISE follows the cycle:

```text
Reflect → Initiate → Sustain → Evolve → new experience
```

Its strategic layers are:

- **Kai / LLM:** conversation, tutoring and reflection;
- **Development Graph:** interests, values, capabilities, difficulties, projects, behavior, feedback and evolution;
- **Assessment Engine:** validated developmental screening, never diagnosis;
- **Intervention Engine:** selects the next action, challenge, format or moment of silence;
- **Character Skills Engine:** turns behavior and evidence into observable capabilities;
- **Safety Engine:** detects risk and governs human escalation;
- **Outcome Engine:** measures what actually helps in each context.

### Kai: the evolving personal character

Kai is the character defined by each young person within RISE. During onboarding and continued use, the young person can shape Kai's communication style, tone, energy, directness, humor, vocabulary and preferred way of giving feedback.

Kai then adapts and evolves with the young person. It learns how to communicate in a way that feels familiar, safe and empathetic, while keeping stable safety boundaries and a consistent commitment to the young person's autonomy.

Kai is not a fixed personality imposed on every user. Each young person should experience a Kai that increasingly understands how they work best. However, Kai must never mirror harmful behavior, reinforce emotional dependency, claim exclusivity or replace real human relationships.

> Kai becomes more familiar with the young person so the young person can become more familiar with themselves.

## V1 experience

The youth receives a safe personal space with Kai to:

- complete a check-in;
- define objectives and commitments;
- record action, reason, deadline, proof, difficulty and fallback plan;
- reflect in a private journal;
- receive consent-based accountability notifications;
- review progress and evidence of capabilities;
- ask for help from trusted people.

The V1 PWA includes login, onboarding, objectives, journal, Kai conversation, progress, notifications and installation on mobile devices.

The backend supports organizations, guardians, researchers and administrators through multi-tenant authentication, consent records, pseudonymized research access, analytics and audit logs.

## Users

- **Youth:** owns goals, reflections, commitments and personal progress.
- **Guardian:** sees approved progress and safety signals, not private conversations by default.
- **Organization administrator:** manages users, invitations, onboarding, consent and operational analytics.
- **Researcher:** accesses authorized pseudonymized data for a declared purpose.
- **Platform administrator:** manages organizations, policies and operational safety.

## Safety and privacy

RISE does not diagnose or replace psychologists, guardians, teachers or emergency services. Crisis, self-harm, abuse, violence and immediate danger interrupt normal coaching, trigger a safe response, create a safety case and route the situation to human support according to policy.

The V1 guardrail is an initial safety layer, not a clinical assessment. Tenant scope comes from authenticated tokens. Research access requires consent, purpose declaration and auditability. Guardians do not receive full conversations by default.

## Success metrics

Success is measured by healthy development, not screen time:

- onboarding and invitation completion;
- time to first goal and first real-world action;
- commitments completed or consciously renegotiated;
- actions initiated outside the app;
- help requests to real people;
- capabilities demonstrated through evidence;
- self-reported clarity and youth satisfaction;
- reduction of dependence on Kai;
- safety events and time to human review.

## V1 scope and roadmap

V1 delivers the controlled pilot: PWA, Kai conversation, objectives, accountability, authentication, onboarding, multi-tenancy, consent, research console, analytics and initial safety controls.

The roadmap adds validated micro-assessments, the Development Graph, real-world experience missions, Character Skills Engine, adaptive intervention engine, independent Safety Engine, Outcome Engine and production Web Push.

V1 deliberately excludes diagnosis, public rankings, social comparison, full guardian surveillance, clinical intervention, peer community and attention-maximizing mechanics.
